# Servee V1 Product Scope

## Product objective

Servee V1 enables a restaurant to manage one complete dine-in journey digitally:

1. A guest scans a table-specific QR code.
2. The guest browses a visual, mobile-first menu.
3. The guest customizes items and submits an order.
4. A waiter reviews and confirms the order.
5. The kitchen receives and progresses the ticket.
6. The guest sees simple status updates and can request service.
7. Staff closes the table after payment.

## Product surfaces

### Guest

- Table session from QR code
- Restaurant and menu landing page
- Menu categories, search, item details, images, dietary labels and allergens
- Item modifiers, notes and cart
- Order submission and status
- Service requests
- Bill request
- English and Spanish localization

### Staff

- Authentication and role-based access
- Assigned tables
- Incoming order review
- Confirm, edit, reject or cancel orders
- Add manual items
- Service-request queue
- Table and check closure

### Kitchen

- Incoming ticket queue
- Station-friendly ticket details
- Modifier, note and allergy visibility
- Accepted, preparing, ready and completed statuses
- Preparation timers

### Restaurant administration

- Restaurant and location setup
- Menu categories and items
- Pricing, modifiers and availability
- Media management
- Table setup and QR-code generation
- Staff and role management
- Basic order and sales reporting

## Explicitly outside initial V1

- Reservations
- Delivery logistics
- Full inventory and procurement
- Payroll and scheduling
- Franchise management
- Dynamic pricing
- Voice ordering
- Advanced loyalty automation
- AI demand forecasting

## Primary success metrics

- Time from seating to submitted order
- Time from submission to staff confirmation
- Order-entry error rate
- Kitchen preparation time
- Digital-order adoption
- Average ticket value
- Time from bill request to table closure
- Guest, waiter and kitchen satisfaction

## V1 acceptance outcome

A pilot restaurant can configure its menu and tables, place a QR code on a real table, process a guest order through staff and kitchen workflows, and close the table with a complete audit trail.
